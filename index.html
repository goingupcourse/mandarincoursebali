Pedagogy & BLCU Curriculum

Beijing Pedagogy.
Practical Bali Application.

At Going Up! (进步), we utilize materials and teaching methods certified by Beijing Language and Culture University (BLCU) — China's premier authority in teaching Chinese as a second language.

Rather than relying strictly on passive exam cramming, our hybrid methodology combines rigorous academic structure with active real-world communication.
